Everything a monthly budget actually needs

💰

Safe-to-spend, always visible

See what's left of your budget the moment you open the app — no digging through spreadsheets.

📊

Category budgets that warn you early

Set a monthly limit per category and get a clear status — on track, almost there, or over — before it's a problem.

🏦

Loan and EMI tracking

Log the real amount and remaining balance on any loan, and pay it off month by month right from the app.

📈

Reports that actually explain your money

Income vs. expense trends, savings rate, and your top spending categories, over 3, 6 or 12 months.

🗂️

Categories that fit how you spend

Start with the built-in categories or add your own — any name, any icon, any color.

⬇️

Your data, exportable anytime

One tap exports everything to CSV — for your own records, your accountant, or a spreadsheet you already use.

🔒

Nothing leaves your device

There's no account, no sign-in, and no server. Every transaction, budget and note is stored locally — private by design, not by policy.
