Privacy Policy

Saving & Spending — last updated September 2026

Saving & Spending is a personal finance tracker created by Asrar Ahmed Junedi. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data when you use the app.

We do not collect your data

Saving & Spending does not have a server, does not use analytics, and does not transmit any of your information anywhere. There is no account creation, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

Where your data actually lives

Everything you enter — your name, transactions, budgets, loans, and settings — is stored only in your own browser's or app's local storage, directly on your device. Nobody else, including the app's creator, can see, access, or receive this data. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, that data is gone — there is no backup copy anywhere else unless you export it yourself (via the CSV export feature in the app).

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, location, microphone, or any other device permission beyond standard local storage, which is required for the app to save your entries between sessions.

Third parties

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) when connected to the internet. No personal or financial data is sent as part of that request — only a standard font file download, the same as visiting any website that uses Google Fonts.

Children's privacy

This app is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age, since it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page.

Contact
